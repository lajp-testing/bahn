The one rule that shapes everything

Below 90% of orders on time, a team is not ranked at all — whatever its margin. So the desk's central question is never “how much can we win?” but “how much profitable work can we still promise?” The final is one secret week at the hardest level: a third of the drivers, unevenly spread between towns, a price war nobody announces, and dealers who trust a team only after on-time deliveries.
