# Ablation: what each idea is worth, 2026-11-15

Track t3. Each variant is the full system (`main`) with ONE idea switched off, played on the 10 dev weeks at level 8 through `bin/vps`, then compared against the full system, label `main@8c7e522` (played by the orchestrator on the same seeds).

Baseline `main@8c7e522`: margin €177,043 over 10 weeks, 401 orders a week, 97.2% mean on time, lowest week 95.5% (dev-04), ranked 10/10.

Note on versions: variant 1 ran on the same code as the baseline (`@8c7e522`). Variants 2–4 ran after commit `2260137` landed in the shared checkout (a demo-inspector/UI change; no dispatcher parameter changed), so their labels carry `@2260137`. Same seeds, same policy otherwise.

## Summary

Bundles are worth the most (−€38,058 without them), then the learned cost model (−€28,301) and the rising fee ladder (−€26,592); the cost tilt is worth the least (−€8,746) but is the steadiest on-time lever, lifting the worst week from 92.8% to 95.5%.
The full system beats every single-idea-off variant on nearly every week (39 of 40 comparisons) and keeps every one of the 10 weeks ranked, above 90% on time in all runs.
Every idea earns its place: none is redundant, and removing any one costs between €8.7k and €38k over ten dev weeks.
