# Ablation: three intake and quoting ideas, dev seeds, 2026-11-15

Each variant is the full system (`--policy main`) with ONE idea switched off,
played on the same 10 dev weeks at level 8, compared against the full system
`main@8c7e522` (played by the orchestrator on the same seeds).

Code versions: variant 1 ran at `8c7e522`; variants 2 and 3 ran at `2260137`.
`git diff 8c7e522..2260137` touches only `demo/` files; `dispatch/`, `harness/`
and `spread/` are identical, so all three comparisons are like for like.

## 1. Intake queue limit off — "take only the work our drivers can deliver"

Label `main[limit=1000,morning_limit=1000,safe_limit=0]@8c7e522`
(`limit=1000`, `morning_limit=1000`, `safe_limit=0`: the limit never binds).

| | full system | limit off | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| total margin, 10 weeks | €177,043 | €177,085 | +€42 |
| orders a week | 401 | 409 | +8 |
| mean on time | 97.2% | 96.2% | −1.0 pt |
| lowest week on time | 95.5% (dev-04) | 92.7% (dev-07) | −2.8 pt |

Full system ahead on 7 of 10 weeks. No week below 90% on time in either
(lowest 92.7%, dev-07 without the idea).

Worth €42 in total margin — nothing — while costing 1 point of mean on time
and pushing dev-07 down to 92.7%, 2.3 points above the ranking floor. The
limit's value is on-time safety, not margin.

## 2. Safe cars off — "past the queue limit, short cars without a private address still get a quote"

Label `main[safe_limit=0]@2260137`.

| | full system | safe cars off | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| total margin, 10 weeks | €177,043 | €173,827 | −€3,216 |
| orders a week | 401 | 390 | −11 |
| mean on time | 97.2% | 96.6% | −0.6 pt |
| lowest week on time | 95.5% (dev-04) | 94.9% (dev-07) | −0.6 pt |

Full system ahead on 7 of 10 weeks. No week below 90% on time in either.

Worth €3,216 over the 10 weeks (€322 a week), plus 11 orders and 0.6 points
of on time. A clear win: the safe-car quotes convert and still arrive.

## 3. Same-day option behind the normal 12 h slack gate

Label `main[same_day_slack=-1]@2260137` (`same_day_slack=-1` switches the
8-hour same-day gate back to the normal 12 h gate).

| | full system | 12 h gate | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| total margin, 10 weeks | €177,043 | €152,203 | −€24,839 |
| orders a week | 401 | 332 | −69 |
| mean on time | 97.2% | 97.5% | +0.3 pt |
| lowest week on time | 95.5% (dev-04) | 95.9% (dev-07) | +0.4 pt |

Full system ahead on 10 of 10 weeks. No week below 90% on time in either.

Worth €24,839 over the 10 weeks (€2,484 a week) and 69 orders a week; the
12 h gate buys back only 0.3 points of on time, which the full system does
not need (97.2% mean, 95.5% lowest, 10/10 ranked). The biggest idea of the
three by an order of magnitude.

## Summary

- Same-day at 8 h slack is the load-bearing idea: removing it costs €24,839
  (−14%) and 69 orders a week across all 10 weeks, for 0.3 points of on time
  the system does not need.
- Safe cars past the queue limit are worth €3,216 (−1.8%) and 11 orders a
  week; removing them also costs 0.6 points of on time.
- The queue limit itself is worth €42 in margin — its value is protective:
  without it dev-07 slides to 92.7% on time, 2.3 points above the ranking
  floor, so keep it for robustness, not for euros.
