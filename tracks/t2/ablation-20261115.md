# Ablation: what each idea is worth — 2026-11-15

Baseline: the full system, `main@8c7e522` (orchestrator's run, same 10 dev weeks, level 8).
Each variant is the full system with ONE idea switched off, played on `--seeds dev` via `bin/vps`, track `t2`.

**Baseline `main@8c7e522`:** total margin €177,043 over 10 weeks, 4,010 orders (401/week), mean on-time 97.2%, lowest week 95.5% (dev-04), ranked 10/10.

## Variant 1 — quoter off: `price=fixed`

Idea removed: "each quote is the price with the most expected margin at the level dealers accept today". `price=fixed` is the kit's fixed markup over the list price instead.

- Label: `main[price=fixed]@8c7e522`
- Total margin: €118,016 without vs €177,043 with → **-€59,027** (full system +50% higher)
- Orders a week: 259 without vs 401 with
- Mean on-time: 93.0% without vs 97.2% with; lowest week 87.1% (dev-10) without vs 95.5% (dev-04) with
- Full system ahead on **10 of 10** weeks
- Weeks below 90% on time: **dev-10 at 87.1%** — that week drops out of the ranking (variant ranked 9/10)

## Variant 2 — dealer trust off: `trust=0`

Idea removed: dealer trust (a dealer accepts 1% more for each order delivered to it on time, and the quoter uses that).

- Label: `main[trust=0]@8c7e522`
- Total margin: €171,617 without vs €177,043 with → **-€5,425**
- Orders a week: 413 without vs 401 with (without trust the quoter prices lower, so slightly more orders land)
- Mean on-time: 96.6% without vs 97.2% with; lowest week 95.2% (dev-07, dev-10) without vs 95.5% (dev-04) with
- Full system ahead on **8 of 10** weeks (trust=0 ahead on dev-01 and dev-03)
- Weeks below 90% on time: none (both ranked 10/10)

## Variant 3 — note reader off: `notes=starter`

Idea removed: the note reader (dealers' free-text notes, read into ready times, hours, deadlines). `notes=starter` is the kit's starter notes.

- Label: `main[notes=starter]@2260137`
- Total margin: €141,823 without vs €177,043 with → **-€35,219**
- Orders a week: 320 without vs 401 with (starter notes misread deadlines, so the desk takes less work it can serve)
- Mean on-time: 97.0% without vs 97.2% with; lowest week 94.6% (dev-09) without vs 95.5% (dev-04) with
- Full system ahead on **10 of 10** weeks
- Weeks below 90% on time: none (both ranked 10/10)

Note: variants 1–2 and the baseline ran at code version `8c7e522`; the repo advanced to `2260137` before variant 3 ran (other tracks committing), so its label carries that version. Same dev seeds throughout.

## Summary

- The margin-maximising quoter is the biggest idea: without it margin drops €59,027 (-33%) and one week (dev-10, 87.1%) falls out of the ranking — keep it.
- The note reader is second: without it margin drops €35,219 (-20%) through lost orders (320 vs 401/week); keep it.
- Dealer trust is worth €5,425 (+3%) and never decides ranking; keep it — small, safe, and it only loses on 2 of 10 weeks.
